# Meridian Marine Expense and Travel Policy

## Submitting expenses
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase through the finance portal with itemised receipts. Claims submitted later than 60 days are rejected.

## Meal limits
Daily meal allowance during business travel is 45 USD. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Client dinners are capped at 80 USD per person and need prior approval from a manager.

## Flights and hotels
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights over 6 hours with director approval. Hotel stays are capped at 180 USD per night in major cities and 120 USD elsewhere.

## Reimbursement timing
Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days to the employee's salary account.
