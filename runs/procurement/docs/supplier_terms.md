# Approved Supplier Terms

## Payment terms
NordMar Parts and Delta Deck Supply are paid net 45 days from invoice. Helix Turbo is paid net 30 days. Aquila Filters and Aquila Safety are paid net 60 days. Early payment within 10 days earns a 2 percent discount with NordMar Parts only.

## Warranty
Engine parts carry a 12 month warranty from installation, capped at 24 months from delivery. Safety equipment carries a 36 month warranty. Wear items such as filters and gaskets carry no warranty beyond manufacturing defects reported within 30 days.

## Returns
Unused parts may be returned within 45 days in original packaging. A restocking fee of 15 percent applies to returns of non-defective items. Custom-ordered parts cannot be returned.

## Urgent delivery
Urgent delivery shortens lead time to 3 working days for catalogue parts in stock and adds a surcharge of 25 percent on the part value. Urgent delivery is not available for items with lead time above 30 days.
