# Meridian Marine Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Shore-based employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; carried days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave must begin within 6 months of the birth or adoption date.

## Requesting leave
Leave requests of up to 5 days must be submitted at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal. Requests longer than 5 days need 30 days notice and approval from the department head.
