# Fleet Procurement Procedure

## Approval limits
Orders up to 2,000 USD may be approved by the Chief Engineer. Orders from 2,001 to 15,000 USD need Superintendent approval. Orders above 15,000 USD need approval from the Technical Director.

## Quotes
Orders above 5,000 USD require at least 3 written quotes. Orders below 5,000 USD from an approved supplier on the catalogue do not need extra quotes.

## Emergency orders
A safety-critical emergency order may be placed without prior approval if the vessel cannot sail safely. The Chief Engineer must report it to the Superintendent within 24 hours and file the paperwork within 5 working days.

## Receiving goods
Goods must be inspected within 48 hours of receipt. Discrepancies are reported to the supplier within 7 days, otherwise the delivery is treated as accepted.
