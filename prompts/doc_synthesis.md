# Prompts used to synthesise the demo documents

The demo documents in `demo_inputs/` were written by an AI assistant (Claude-style chat model) from these prompts. They are fictional.

1. HR demo: "Write three short HR policy documents for a fictional shipping company, Meridian Marine: leave policy, remote and hybrid work policy, expense and travel policy. Use markdown headings, concrete numbers (days, amounts, notice periods), and a few exceptions. 300-500 words each."
2. Procurement demo: "Write a ship-spares catalogue CSV (part_id, description, unit_price_usd, lead_time_days, supplier, min_order_qty, 20 rows of marine engine and deck spares), a supplier terms document (payment terms, warranty, returns, urgent delivery surcharge) and an internal procurement procedure (approval limits, quotes needed, emergency orders)."
