# Opening hours
Harbour Vet Clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00 and on Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. The clinic is closed on Sunday and on public holidays.

# Booking appointments
Routine appointments can be booked by phone or through the client portal. A standard consultation lasts 20 minutes and costs $55. A long consultation lasts 40 minutes and costs $95.
Clients must cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment. A late cancellation or a missed appointment is charged a $25 fee.

# Emergencies
Emergencies are seen during opening hours without an appointment. Outside opening hours, clients must call the emergency line 555-0142, which is answered by the partner hospital Bayside Animal Emergency. An out-of-hours emergency call-out fee of $150 applies.
