# IT Helpdesk Services

## Support hours
The helpdesk is open Monday to Friday from 8 AM to 6 PM. Urgent outages outside these hours are handled by the on-call engineer through the emergency line.

## Software requests
New software requests are reviewed within 5 working days. Software over 500 USD needs approval from the department head. Open source tools on the approved list can be installed without a request.

## Hardware refresh
Laptops are replaced every 4 years. Monitors are replaced only when faulty. Accessories such as keyboards are issued on request.
