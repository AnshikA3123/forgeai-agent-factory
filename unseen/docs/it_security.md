# IT Security Guide

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and changed every 90 days. Reusing any of the last 8 passwords is not allowed. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for VPN and email.

## Lost devices
A lost laptop or phone must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours. The helpdesk will remotely wipe the device within 1 hour of the report.

## Phishing
Suspicious emails should be forwarded to phishing@example.test and then deleted. Never enter credentials on a link received by email.
