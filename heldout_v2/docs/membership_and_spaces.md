# Membership and Spaces

## Membership
Residents of Harbour City join free with proof of address. Non-residents pay 30 EUR per year. Children under 14 need a parent or guardian to sign the form. A replacement card costs 3 EUR.

## Opening hours
The library opens Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 20:00, Saturday from 10:00 to 17:00, and is closed on Sunday. On public holidays it is closed.

## Study rooms
Study rooms seat 4 to 6 people. Members can book a room for up to 2 hours per day, and up to 7 days ahead. Rooms are booked at the front desk or through the member portal. Food is not allowed in study rooms, but covered drinks are fine.

## Printing and computers
Printing costs 0.10 EUR per black and white page and 0.50 EUR per colour page. Public computers can be used for 60 minutes per session, and a session can be extended once if nobody is waiting.
