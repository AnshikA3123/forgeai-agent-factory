# Harbour City Library Borrowing Rules

## Loan periods
Books may be borrowed for 21 days. DVDs and audiobooks may be borrowed for 7 days. Reference works and newspapers cannot leave the building.

## Renewals
Items can be renewed twice, for 14 days each time, unless another member has reserved them. Renewals can be made online, by phone on 555-0142, or at the front desk.

## Fines
Overdue books cost 0.25 EUR per day, capped at 10 EUR per item. DVDs cost 1 EUR per day, capped at 15 EUR. Borrowing is blocked once a member owes 20 EUR or more.

## Lost items
A lost book is charged at its replacement price plus a 5 EUR handling fee. The handling fee is waived if the item is returned within 60 days.
