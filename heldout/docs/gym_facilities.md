# Facilities and Classes

## Class booking
Group classes can be booked up to 7 days ahead through the app. A booking must be cancelled at least 2 hours before the class, otherwise a 5 USD no-show charge applies. Classes hold a maximum of 20 people.

## Lockers
Day lockers are free but must be emptied by closing time. Monthly lockers cost 10 USD per month and are allocated on a first come basis.

## Towels and showers
Towels are provided at the Premium front desk only. Showers are open to all members until 30 minutes after closing.
