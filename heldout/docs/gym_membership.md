# Harbor Fitness Membership Rules

## Membership plans
The Basic plan costs 30 USD per month and includes gym floor access from 6 AM to 10 PM. The Premium plan costs 55 USD per month and adds group classes, sauna access and 2 guest passes per month.

## Freezing and cancelling
Members may freeze a membership for up to 3 months per year for a fee of 5 USD per month. Cancellation needs 30 days written notice and takes effect at the end of the billing cycle. There is no refund for unused days.

## Joining fee
A one-time joining fee of 40 USD applies to new members. The fee is waived during the January promotion.
