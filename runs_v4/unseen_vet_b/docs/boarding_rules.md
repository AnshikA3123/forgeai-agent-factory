# Boarding eligibility
Dogs and cats may board at the clinic. Rabbits may board only in the small-animal room. Every boarding pet must be at least 12 weeks old.
Dogs must have a valid kennel cough vaccination given at least 10 days before check-in. Cats must have a valid cat flu vaccination.

# Boarding fees
Boarding costs $40 per night for a dog and $28 per night for a cat. A second pet from the same household receives a 15 percent discount. A deposit of 30 percent is required at booking and is not refunded if the booking is cancelled less than 7 days before check-in.

# Check-in and check-out
Check-in is between 8:00 and 10:00. Check-out is before 17:00. Pets collected after 17:00 are charged for an extra night.
Clients must bring the pet's own food. The clinic supplies a standard diet for $6 per day if no food is brought.
