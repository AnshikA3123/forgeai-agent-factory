# Meridian Marine Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility
Shore staff may work remotely after completing their 3-month probation period. Roles that require physical presence in the port office, such as security and facilities, are not eligible.

## Hybrid schedule
The standard hybrid arrangement is 3 days in the office and 2 days remote each week. Tuesday and Wednesday are mandatory in-office days for all teams.

## Equipment and allowance
The company provides a laptop and monitor. Remote workers receive a home-office allowance of 40 USD per month, paid with the monthly salary. Internet costs are not reimbursed separately.

## Working abroad
Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year with written manager approval. Longer stays need approval from the HR director and may have tax consequences that the employee must clear independently.
